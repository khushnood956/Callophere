CMS

CMS Guidelines & Audit Readiness

  • TPMO Disclaimer Integration: Standardized delivery of mandatory TPMO disclaimers within required call timeframes.
  • Scope of Appointment (SOA): Workflow rules enforcing SOA completion prior to plan presentation where required.
  • 100% Call Recording Archival: Secure cloud audio recording retrieval structured for audit readiness.
  • Secret Shopper Preparedness: Internal QA evaluations modeled after regulatory monitoring criteria.
HIPAA

HIPAA, TCPA & Consent Governance

  • HIPAA Privacy Controls: Personnel trained in Protected Health Information (PHI) handling under strict role-based access limits.
  • TCPA Process Compliance: Prior Express Written Consent (PEWC) documentation for outbound interactions.
  • Broker Consent Verification: Documented one-to-one consent workflows for ACA Marketplace interactions.
  • Quality Monitoring & Coaching: Regular call scoring, script adherence checks, and mandatory retraining.

Operational Compliance Note: Callophere implements operational controls, quality monitoring, and agent training designed to support client compliance objectives. Specific legal and regulatory responsibilities depend on the client's business model, applicable contracts, state licensing laws, and governing regulations. Callophere does not claim independent HIPAA certification or official CMS approval, as CMS does not certify third-party BPO vendors directly.
